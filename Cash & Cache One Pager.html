Key Takeaways
01 · The Bridge
Cowork closes the gap between "cool demo" and real utility — a terminal-based agent that reads files, executes multi-step tasks, and handles work that previously needed a developer.
02 · Real Production
10 creators, 12 live workflows across 6 categories — systems running real businesses. None are demos. All break if the automation fails.
03 · Folder Context
Persistent folder memory is the unlock — brand voice, prior outputs, and project structure turn one-off prompts into repeatable, compounding systems.
04 · The Real ROI
The win is consistency, not speed. Automating repetitive high-stakes tasks removes the friction that causes creators and teams to skip steps and lose momentum.
